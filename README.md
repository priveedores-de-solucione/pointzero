# Point Zero

### Where builders begin.

Point Zero is a physical technology garage and builder community for ambitious young people building the future, starting from places the world isn't watching.

This repository holds the **Point Zero web platform**: the website and online home where builders apply, join programs, meet each other, find mentors, showcase what they're making and connect with investors.

---

## The idea

**Talent is everywhere. Opportunity isn't.**

There are brilliant developers writing code from bedrooms, designers building products from university hostels, and founders working on ideas nobody around them understands. Too often they're told to leave their city, their university or their home to be taken seriously.

Point Zero disagrees. Great things can be built from overlooked places, and what many talented people need isn't another course or certificate. They need **a place to build.**

### Why "Point Zero"?

Zero isn't nothing. It's the starting coordinate, the place from which you measure distance. Point Zero is where someone arrives as a developer and leaves as a founder, arrives looking for a community and leaves with a co-founder, or arrives with an idea nobody takes seriously and leaves with a company.

> Your starting point can become your turning point.

---

## What Point Zero is (and isn't)

| Point Zero **is** | Point Zero is **not** |
|---|---|
| A garage where serious things get built | Another coworking space full of desks |
| A community of people who ship | A coding academy chasing certificates |
| A launchpad from idea to company | An events centre in disguise |
| A place where people help each other | A networking club that swaps LinkedIn profiles |

---

## Who belongs here

**Builders.** That's the simplest answer.

Developers, designers, founders, AI engineers, researchers, product people, blockchain and cybersecurity practitioners, hardware hackers, robotics enthusiasts, creative technologists, and students already building beyond their classrooms.

You don't need a startup, funding or a famous degree. You need **curiosity, ambition and the willingness to build.**

---

## What we believe

- **Build > talk.** We respect the person who ships the ugly first version more than the person with the perfect pitch deck.
- **Talent doesn't need permission.** A young developer in Ogbomoso should be able to build for the world.
- **Small places can build big things.** Small communities move fast, and small teams can challenge giants.
- **Competition outside, collaboration inside.** Two founders can compete and still help each other.
- **Failure is data.** Things will break. That's what building looks like.
- **Leave better than you arrived.** Mentor, teach, introduce, hire, invest. Every generation makes the next one stronger.

---

## The Point Zero journey

Every member moves through a path:

| Stage | What it feels like |
|---|---|
| **Explore** | You're curious, meeting people, experimenting and finding your crowd. |
| **Build** | You have something worth pursuing, find collaborators and ship. |
| **Launch** | It's real and people can use it. Now come the harder questions: who needs this, and how do we grow? |
| **Scale** | You have traction and need talent, capital, customers, partners and mentors. |
| **Graduate** | You outgrow the garage, build something massive, then come back to mentor, invest and hire. |

The goal is to produce people who eventually make Point Zero unnecessary for themselves, and who then return to make it necessary for someone else.

## The garage (the physical space)

The space is designed to feel like a garage where serious things happen, not an office.

- **The Garage:** open collaborative workspace
- **The Lab:** hardware, AI, robotics and electronics
- **The War Room:** for teams going deep on hard problems
- **The Commons:** food, conversation and accidental collaborations
- **The Studio:** content, podcasts, livestreams and demos
- **The Quiet Room:** for focused, serious work
- **The Stage:** demos, launches, talks and debates
- **The Residency** (eventually): an intense, selected period of building, with a place to live, a place to work and one expectation: *build something*

## What this platform does

The website is the online home of the community. It has four areas.

### 1. The public website
Open to everyone. Visitors can learn about Point Zero, browse **programs**, **events**, **resources** and the **startups** being built, meet the **community**, see the **campus**, explore **partnerships** and **investor** information, get in touch, or **apply** to join.

### 2. The member dashboard
For founders, builders, students, mentors and partners. Members can:
- build their personal profile
- create and manage their **startup** page, team and progress
- join **programs** and **cohorts**
- track **milestones** from idea to launch
- request **mentorship** and book sessions with mentors
- register for **events** and access **resources**
- **message** other members and receive **notifications**

### 3. The investor portal
For verified investors. Investors can:
- discover startups coming out of Point Zero
- save startups they're interested in
- request **introductions** to founders (reviewed by the Point Zero team first)
- track their deal flow

### 4. The admin console
For the Point Zero team. Staff can:
- review and decide on **applications**
- manage users, startups, programs, cohorts, mentors, investors and partners
- publish events and resources and send notifications
- see **analytics** on how the community is doing
- keep an **audit log** of important actions

## Who uses the platform

| Role | What they do |
|---|---|
| **Founder** | Runs a startup and builds a team |
| **Builder** | Works on projects and collaborates |
| **Student** | Learns by building and meets future co-founders |
| **Mentor** | Guides members through sessions and feedback |
| **Investor** | Finds promising startups and requests introductions |
| **Partner** | Organisations that sponsor, hire or collaborate |
| **Staff / Admin** | The Point Zero team that runs the community |

When someone signs up, they choose how they'll use Point Zero (founder, builder, student, mentor, investor or partner). Team roles are assigned only by admins. Investors must be verified before they can see startups.

## How we measure success

Not by how many people walked through the door, but by what happened because they did:

- Products shipped
- Companies started
- Founders who found co-founders
- People who got their first opportunity
- Jobs created, revenue generated and capital raised
- Open-source projects launched
- Alumni who came back to help others

And eventually: **how many world-class companies started here.**
